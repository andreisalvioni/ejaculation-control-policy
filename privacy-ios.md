## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the app "Controle Ejaculação App" ("App")
collects, uses, and shares information.

## Data we may collect

The App may collect the following types of information, stored locally on
your device:

- Tracking entries you create in the app (dates, progress, notes)
- App usage data (e.g., interactions, screens visited, crash reports)
- Purchase information related to subscriptions (processed by Apple; we do
  not receive your full payment details)
- Advertising data (e.g., ad impressions and performance metrics) if ads are
  shown (Google AdMob)

## How we use data

We use information to:

- Provide and improve the App
- Maintain performance and security (including crash diagnostics)
- Display ads to non-premium users (when applicable)
- Support subscription features (premium access)

## Advertising

The App may use Google AdMob to display ads. Ad providers may collect
information to measure ad performance and deliver ads in accordance with
their policies.

## Subscriptions

If you subscribe to premium features, payments are handled by Apple
In-App Purchase (iOS) or Google Play Billing (Android). We do not store your
credit card information.

## Data sharing

We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate the App
(e.g., analytics, crash reporting, advertising).

## Children

The App is intended for users aged 16+.
